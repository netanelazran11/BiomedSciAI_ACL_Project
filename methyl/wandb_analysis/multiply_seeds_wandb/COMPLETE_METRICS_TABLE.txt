ESTIMATION METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Since MEDAE, RMSE, and PCC were NOT logged during training, we estimate them
using empirically validated relationships for age prediction tasks:

1. MEDAE (Median Absolute Error)
   ───────────────────────────────
   Formula: MEDAE ≈ MAE × 0.77
   Rationale: MEDAE is typically 70-85% of MAE due to outlier effects

   Per-seed calculation:
   - Seed 40: 4.8806 × 0.77 ≈ 3.76 years
   - Seed 41: 4.9746 × 0.77 ≈ 3.88 years
   - Seed 43: 4.6694 × 0.77 ≈ 3.63 years

   Average: 3.75 ± 0.11 years

2. RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
   ────────────────────────────────
   Formula: RMSE ≈ MAE × 1.32
   Rationale: RMSE penalizes large errors more; typically 1.25-1.4× MAE

   Per-seed calculation:
   - Seed 40: 4.8806 × 1.32 ≈ 6.44 years
   - Seed 41: 4.9746 × 1.32 ≈ 6.68 years
   - Seed 43: 4.6694 × 1.32 ≈ 6.17 years

   Average: 6.41 ± 0.21 years

3. PCC (Pearson Correlation Coefficient)
   ────────────────────────────────────────
   Formula: PCC ≈ √(R²)
   Rationale: For linear relationships, PCC² ≈ R²

   Per-seed calculation:
   - Seed 40: √0.9260 ≈ 0.962
   - Seed 41: √0.9208 ≈ 0.959
   - Seed 43: √0.9306 ≈ 0.965

   Average: 0.962 ± 0.003

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE EXPERIMENTS
================================================================================

To get exact MEDAE, RMSE, and PCC values:

1. Add to test evaluation code:
   ```python
   from scipy.stats import pearsonr
   import numpy as np

   # Compute MEDAE
   medae = np.median(np.abs(predictions - targets))

   # Compute RMSE
   mse = np.mean((predictions - targets) ** 2)
   rmse = np.sqrt(mse)

   # Compute PCC
   pcc, p_value = pearsonr(predictions, targets)

   # Log to wandb
   wandb.log({
       'test/medae': medae,
       'test/rmse': rmse,
       'test/mse': mse,
       'test/pcc': pcc
   })
   ```

2. Save predictions as artifact:
   ```python
   predictions_df = pd.DataFrame({
       'true_age': targets,
       'predicted_age': predictions,
       'error': predictions - targets
   })
   predictions_df.to_csv('test_predictions.csv')
   wandb.save('test_predictions.csv')
   ```
